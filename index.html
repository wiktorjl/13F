Scope and limitations

Form 13F reports gross-long shares and options held. It omits short stock and written options. Quarterly snapshots incorporate restatements and later new-holdings amendments. Filing-table totals are reconciled to SEC summaries; discrepancies remain browseable but are excluded from complete-filing comparisons.

How the fund signal is estimated

The signal infers split-normalized SH-unit changes from complete adjacent-quarter 13F filings, then measures each change from the first market close strictly after its filing through one common latest daily close. Increases and new positions are treated as long exposure; reductions and exits are treated as avoided exposure versus cash. Signal return and Signal P&L are hypothetical research measures—not actual performance, trade dates, sale proceeds, or cost basis—and exclude dividends and trading costs. Funds are ranked only when at least 10 signals are priced, event coverage is at least 80%, and effective bets are at least 5. Price source and latest close date load with the fund data.

About the $1B research-universe cutoff

The default includes managers with at least $1 billion in latest disclosed 13F value plus every Featured manager. This is a browsing heuristic—not firm AUM or a quality judgment—and All filers keeps every manager available. Exact fund searches check the full directory. Obvious legacy-thousands value submissions are normalized for cutoff eligibility only; displayed Reported value remains as filed.

About the featured research set

This editorial set is a research shortcut, not a performance ranking or investment advice. It combines current manager prominence and earnings from Institutional Investor's 2026 Rich List, scale from With Intelligence's H1 2025 top-AUM list, and widely followed 13F equity managers from HedgeTrace's 2026 manager list. Only active filers in this local dataset were selected, and exact SEC CIKs—not fuzzy names—determine inclusion. A 13F is delayed and does not show a manager's complete strategy.

How quarterly changes and market cap are calculated

Each release uses the paired cohort of managers with complete filings in both adjacent quarters. Security numerators and unit-change breadth use only rows reported with the SH amount type; PRN and other amount types remain available in Positions and detail views but are excluded from this ranking. Reported value change sums signed changes in end-quarter position value and includes price movement; it is not a trade or share-count measure. Portfolio weight change measures the percentage-point change in each ranked security's share of the cohort's full reported 13F portfolio, including PRN and other positions in the denominator. Change vs. prior value divides the release's value change by its prior reported position value; a zero prior position is unavailable, while a full exit is −100%. Multi-release change is a sum for the additive value and portfolio-weight modes, and a prior-value-weighted percentage for change vs. prior value. Zero-base releases are excluded from the latter; defined exits remain included. Trend sorting uses the latest defined release less the earliest defined release and requires two defined releases. Funds increasing and decreasing count managers whose reported units rose or fell in the latest release; their filters are inclusive. Market capitalization comes from a timestamped Nasdaq Stock Screener snapshot matched by ticker; it is current snapshot data, not historical quarter-end market cap. Unavailable market caps display as an em dash and remain browseable unless a market-cap bound is applied. These are reported-position changes, not confirmed trades.
